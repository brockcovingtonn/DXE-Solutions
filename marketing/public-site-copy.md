### What we do

**Space planning.** We set your existing conditions up as a working plan, then
develop layout options against your program — how many bedrooms, where the
kitchen wants to be, what the circulation costs you. You get alternatives, not one
take-it-or-leave-it drawing.

**Design development.** Dimensioned final plans, furniture and fixture layout, and
material and finish direction at the level your project needs.

**Visualization.** A complete 3D model of the design and photorealistic interior and
exterior views, so the decision is made from something you can see rather than
something you have to imagine.

### Who it's for

- **Homeowners** planning a kitchen, primary suite, whole-floor or whole-house remodel.
- **ADU and garage conversion projects**, where the real problem is fitting an entire
  living program into limited square footage.
- **Realtors** marketing a property on its potential rather than its current condition.
- **Contractors, developers and interior designers** who need presentation-quality
  plans and renderings on a repeatable basis. Ask about trade partner rates.

### How it works

1. **Intake.** Tell us the project type, the approximate area and what you have
   already — existing plans, a survey, photos, or nothing at all.
2. **Proposal.** A fixed package price within one business day. No hourly surprises.
3. **Concepts.** Layout options to react to, then a round of revisions.
4. **Delivery.** Final dimensioned plans, 3D model and rendered views as a
   presentation-ready package.

### The part most studios leave vague

Northlight Studio produces conceptual design and visualization documents. They are
not stamped architectural, structural or MEP drawings and they are not permit or
construction documents.

That is a deliberate boundary, and it is also why the studio sits where it does.
When your project reaches the point of needing permits, grading, drainage or
entitlements, DXE Solutions — a civil engineering and construction project
management firm serving Greater Los Angeles and Ventura County — can take it from
there under its own scope. One relationship, the right credential at each stage.

## Language to avoid

- "Architectural plans", "blueprints", "permit-ready", "construction documents",
  "stamped", "sealed", "engineered" — all of these imply licensed work.
- "Unlimited revisions."
- Published per-square-foot pricing. Square footage is an internal input; the client
  sees a package price.
